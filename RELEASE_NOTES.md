# Pip 1.11.0

A new creature moved into the notch, and she brought her own furniture.

## New

- **Capri.** Pick her in Settings → Appearance and the whole notch becomes
  hers: her faces from *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* instead of a drawn creature,
  changing with the mood, hard-edged plates with a violet shadow, her fonts,
  titles that decode in, the odd glitch, and a lot of opinions about your
  builds. Settings and the activity panel dress up to match. Her Crimson coat
  and Night City notch theme come with her.
- **Her HUD.** While Capri is picked, the line along the bottom of the notch
  shows how full Claude's context is, failed tool calls in a row earn wanted
  stars beside the title, and when Claude finishes while you're in another
  window the notch rings: **Answer →** takes you to that terminal.
- **She roams.** Turn on **Roam the desktop when idle** (in General, once
  she's picked) and while Claude has nothing going on she leaves the notch to
  peek in from the edge of your screen: small, half hidden, saying whatever
  comes to mind. Bring your pointer close and she's off somewhere else; the
  moment Claude gets busy she's back.

## Nothing changes unless you pick her

Every other creature works exactly as it did in 1.10.0: no gauge, no stars,
no calls, no roaming, and her colours aren't in the picker. Pick anyone else
and she steps aside.

Capri's portraits and figure are frames from *Cyberpunk: Edgerunners* and
belong to their rights holders; they aren't covered by Pip's MIT license.

## Install

Pip finds this one itself and offers **Restart now**.

Otherwise download `Pip-Setup-1.11.0.exe` below and run it once; it installs for
your user only. The installer isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may
warn you: choose **More info → Run anyway**.

Requires Windows 10 or 11 (x64).
